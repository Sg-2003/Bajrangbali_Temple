💡 Saturday & Tuesday Special Timings

On holy Tuesdays and Saturdays, the temple gates remain open continuously from 05:30 AM to 09:30 PM. Special congregational Sundarkand Path chanting is organized on Saturdays at 05:30 PM, followed by the grand Sandhya Aarti. Panchamukhi Hanuman Archana is offered throughout the day.
