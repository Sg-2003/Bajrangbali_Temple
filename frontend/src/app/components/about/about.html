About Our Temple

The Legacy of Devotion and Divine Grace in Kalikapur, Jharkhand

History & Significance

The Bajrangbali Hanuman Mandir, located in the peaceful village of Kalikapur within the Potka Block of East Singhbhum district, Jharkhand, stands as a symbol of eternal faith, devotion, and community unity.

For decades, the mandir has been a spiritual sanctuary for locals and travelers passing through Potka. Founded by devout elders of the village, it started as a small shrine under a sacred Banyan tree. Over the years, driven by the profound faith and contributions of devotees, it has transformed into a beautiful temple complex.

The temple houses a magnificent idol of Lord Hanuman in a standing posture, holding his Gada (mace) and offering blessings to all who enter. Devotees believe that praying here with an honest heart removes all obstacles, fear, and negativity from their lives.

📍 Potka, Kalikapur, Jharkhand

Virtues of Lord Hanuman

Channelling the divine traits of Bajrangbali in our daily lives

💪

Infinite Strength (Bala)

Hanuman represents strength that is disciplined and dedicated entirely to the service of righteousness (Dharma).

🪔

Absolute Devotion (Bhakti)

His absolute surrender and love for Lord Rama teaches us the beauty of complete faith, selflessness, and spiritual surrender.

📖

Supreme Wisdom (Jnana)

Despite his immense physical power, Hanuman is the master of scriptures and languages, showing us that strength must always be guided by intellect and wisdom.

Temple Activities & Seva

Beyond daily prayers and rituals, the Bajrangbali Hanuman Mandir serves as a vibrant community hub for Vill-Kalikapur. We regularly conduct welfare and cultural activities:

  • Bhandara & Prasad Vitran: Free community meals served to everyone, especially on Hanuman Jayanti and major festivals.
  • Social Support: Providing assistance to local students, supporting village health camps, and maintaining a clean green environment around the temple premises.

Weekly congregation

Every Saturday evening, hundreds of devotees from nearby villages gather to chant the Hanuman Chalisa 11 times collectively, creating an atmosphere charged with spiritual energy.
